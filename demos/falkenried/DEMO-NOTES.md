# Falkenried demo

Fictional portfolio business. No association with the source business is claimed.

- Build: `npm run build`
- Output: `dist/`, mount at `/demos/falkenried/`
- 45 static German/English pages; base-path rewrite is part of the build.
- Contact forms are local interactive demonstrations and never transmit data.
- The former company video, real booking service and live vehicle iframe were removed.
- All pages are noindex; business schema is omitted for the fictional demo.
- Green/cream editorial homepage, custom wordmark, mobile navigation and reduced-motion support.

Source path: Demo Website/Falkenried_Website/falkenried-website.
