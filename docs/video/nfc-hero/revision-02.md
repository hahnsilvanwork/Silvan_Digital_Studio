## Locked creative requirements

One fixed camera, one lighting setup, one phone, one persistent holding hand. Phone remains visibly held, upright, screen readable, throughout the interaction and all five examples. It never stands on the floor, leans on a product, disappears, becomes transparent or changes model.

The card and phone remain separate objects with a visible gap. A physical product carries only its original printed artwork. App interfaces belong exclusively inside the phone screen. Keep the top of the held phone close enough to the card to make the NFC interaction understandable.
