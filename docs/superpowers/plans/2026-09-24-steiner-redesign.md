## Design contract

Grounded directions: architecture monograph, project dossier, material library, residential magazine, Swiss architecture exhibition, workshop, renovation route. Concept seed assigned direction 5: Swiss architecture exhibition. Limestone paper, forest green type/actions, sage surfaces, large truthful imagery, regular Barlow typography and horizontal navigation. Room imagery leads; craft imagery explains the approach. Preserve all four service areas and anchor routes.

Challengers rejected as unsuitable metaphors: arcade/CRT, film bench, blacksmith, silk canopy, orienteering map, chaotic surf magazine. Retain disciplined states, traceable selection, craft context, whitespace, orientation and image scale. The user delegated creative choices and release, so no additional design approval is necessary.

Key mechanism: a service/project choice carries into a locally reviewable enquiry. Edit/reset, accessible validation, no sending or storage, disabled without JavaScript. No fabricated credentials/customer projects; no trackers or remote fonts. Responsive navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, noindex and deployment CSP required.
